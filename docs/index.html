02 SHOWThree style frames rendered with your real title. You pick one.
03 PLANA storyboard with voice lines, focal points and the word each hit syncs to.
04 BUILDCanvas scenes plus Edge TTS word timings. The agent reviews its own probe frames.
05 VERIFYParallel render and a mix normalised to −16 LUFS. cv check reports A/V sync, captions and duration.
